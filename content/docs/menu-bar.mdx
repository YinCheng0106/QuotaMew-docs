QuotaMew lives in the macOS menu bar. Click its status item to open the dashboard; use the native right-click menu for **Refresh Now**, **Settings…**, and **Quit QuotaMew**.

![QuotaMew macOS menu bar dashboard showing Codex 5-hour, Weekly, and Luna Reserve quota](/images/screenshots/quotamew-dashboard.png)

## Menu Bar display

In **Single** mode, choose one quota to display: **5-hour**, **Weekly**, or **Luna Reserve**. In **Overview** mode, QuotaMew can show both regular windows together, for example `5H 86% · W 71%`.

- `5H` — 5-hour quota
- `W` — Weekly quota
- `R` — Luna Reserve

**Remaining** and **Used** are presentation modes. For example, Remaining may show `5H 86% · W 71%`, while Used may show `5H 14% · W 29%`. Switching them does not change provider quota data.

Luna Reserve is an additional quota bucket observed from Codex data and presented conservatively. You may select it explicitly in Single mode. In Overview mode it appears only when QuotaMew's conservative prominence rule allows it. QuotaMew does not control, activate, or guarantee Reserve routing.

The General settings screen controls how quota values are presented, which provider is pinned to the menu bar, and whether Single or Overview mode is used.

![QuotaMew settings showing Remaining and Used presentation plus Single and Overview menu bar modes](/images/screenshots/quotamew-settings.png)

## Onboarding and Settings

The first-run onboarding briefly explains provider status, privacy, quota presentation, provider preference, Launch at Login, and optional notifications. It is not an account-creation or provider-installation wizard. You can replay onboarding from Settings.

Notification permission is requested only after you explicitly choose the notification action. Notifications are optional and are delivered locally by macOS.
